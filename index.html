About This Position

📋 Job Overview

We're seeking a highly organized and detail-oriented Administrative Personal Assistant to support an accomplished entrepreneur and investment professional. This is a flexible, remote position ideal for students and professionals seeking quality part-time work.

🎯 Key Responsibilities

📅 Organization & Scheduling

Manage calendars, coordinate daily activities, and plan travel arrangements

💼 Account Management

Track accounts, maintain detailed records, and manage financial documentation

📧 Email & Communication

Organize emails, manage archives, handle correspondence, and answer calls

🗂️ Document Organization

File, organize, and maintain document systems with meticulous attention to detail

🔍 Research & Support

Conduct internet research, compile reports, and provide project support

🚀 General Support

Handle office errands and provide administrative assistance as needed

✅ Required Qualifications

  • 1-2+ years of administrative or personal assistant experience
  • Excellent organizational and time management skills
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office and Google Suite
  • Professional communication skills (verbal & written)
  • Reliable, independent work ethic
  • Professional home workspace with reliable internet
  • US-based with legal authorization to work

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous administrative experience? +

We prefer 1-2+ years of relevant experience, but we value strong organizational skills and willingness to learn. If you have excellent time management and attention to detail, we encourage you to apply!

Is this position truly remote? +

Yes! This is 100% remote. You can work from home, a coffee shop, school, or anywhere with a reliable internet connection. We only require that you have a quiet, professional workspace during work hours.

Can I work part-time while in school? +

Absolutely! This position is designed with flexibility in mind. Many of our ideal candidates are students looking to build professional experience while managing their coursework. Work 5-30 hours per week—your choice!

What's the application timeline? +

Here's our typical timeline: (1) Submit application, (2) Initial review: 2-3 business days, (3) Phone interview: within 1 week if selected, (4) Formal interview: if applicable, (5) Offer and onboarding: immediate upon acceptance.

When do benefits start? +

Medical coverage becomes available after 1 month of employment (includes a pay increase), and 401(k) eligibility begins after 3 months (also includes a pay increase). AD & D Insurance is available from day one.

What software do I need to know? +

Proficiency with Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook) and/or Google Suite (Docs, Sheets, Gmail) is essential. We'll teach you any specific tools we use. We value your ability to learn quickly more than your previous experience with niche software.

Can I increase my hours after starting? +

Yes! Many employees start part-time and later increase their hours as they become more familiar with the role and our needs align. Flexibility goes both ways.

Do you hire contractors or employees? +

We work with both W-2 employees and independent contractors (1099). This will be determined during the offer stage based on your needs and our requirements.

What if I have more questions before applying? +

Great question! You can email us with any questions before submitting your application. We're happy to clarify anything about the position, requirements, or process.
