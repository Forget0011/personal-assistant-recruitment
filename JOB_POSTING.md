### Organization & Time Management
- Manage and maintain calendars, schedules, and daily activity plans
- Coordinate travel arrangements and logistics
- Prioritize tasks and manage multiple deadlines simultaneously
- Ensure timely completion of all assigned projects

### Financial & Account Management
- Track and manage accounts with strict attention to detail
- Maintain accurate, detailed records and notes
- Assist with expense tracking and documentation
- Organize financial correspondence and records

### Administrative Support
- Manage email systems and digital archives
- Organize and maintain filing systems (digital and physical)
- Handle correspondence and communication
- Answer phones and manage incoming requests
- Research relevant information and compile reports

### General Support
- Execute general office errands and tasks
- Provide project-based administrative support as needed
- Assist with event coordination and planning
- Support various business initiatives and ventures
